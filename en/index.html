About Us

About Cirebon Smart Farm

A hydroponic farm in Cirebon built on measurement rather than guesswork

Farming Where Every Number Is Measured

We grow melon and leafy greens without soil, in a greenhouse where temperature, humidity and nutrient strength are controlled rather than left to the weather. Every tank is logged, which is what makes a harvest repeatable instead of lucky.

Automatic Dosing

Nutrients are metered to a target EC, not poured by eye, so every plant in a run gets the same feed

Humidity Control

Fans hold humidity and keep air moving, which is the cheapest defense against fungal disease

Microbubble Aeration

Fine bubbles raise dissolved oxygen at the roots, where root rot usually starts

Live Temperature Monitoring

Greenhouse temperature is watched continuously, because warm water turns lettuce bitter long before it looks wrong
