About Cirebon Smart Farm

A farm run by numbers, not guesswork

Home / About

Cirebon Smart Farm is a modern hydroponic farm in Cirebon, West Java. We grow premium Hami melons and fresh lettuce inside a greenhouse where temperature, humidity, and nutrients are controlled by an automated system. We are now preparing the next line: tilapia farming with a biofloc system.

Why We Farm This Way

Farming in Indonesia often loses out not because the soil lacks fertility, but because the results are hard to predict. The weather changes, pests arrive, and harvest quality goes up and down with no pattern. Large buyers need certainty, and certainty only comes from data.

So we built a farm where every variable is measured. Nutrients are delivered through a dosing system at a precise dose. Humidity and air circulation are set by automatic fans. Greenhouse temperature is monitored continuously. Microbubble aeration maintains oxygen levels in the root zone. It all comes down to one thing: harvests that are uniform, repeatable, and can be promised to buyers.

We are now bringing the same approach to water. A biofloc system lets us raise fish at high density with water parameters measured every day, exactly the way we treat the nutrient solution in the hydroponic growing channels.

What We Stand By

Four things that determine every decision on the farm

Measured

Every parameter is recorded, from solution pH to daily temperature. Decisions come from numbers, not from habit.

Sustainable

Water is reused, waste is processed back into nutrients, and chemical use is kept as low as possible.

Honest With Buyers

We only promise what we can actually deliver. If a product is not ready, we say it is not ready.

Open Sharing

Our technical notes are open to other farmers. Modern farming grows faster when the knowledge is not kept to yourself.

Our Farm's Journey

Built in stages, one system at a time

  1. Stage 1 — Hydroponic Basics

    The first growing channel setup and NFT system were built. One focus: making sure the nutrient solution flows steadily and the roots get enough oxygen.

  2. Stage 2 — Greenhouse Automation

    An automatic dosing system, humidity control fans, and real-time temperature monitoring were installed. From this point on, harvest results started to become predictable.

  3. Stage 3 — Premium Products

    Hami melons and hydroponic lettuce reached a consistent quality standard, with post-harvest handling and packaging that is food grade.

  4. Stage 4 — Microbubble Technology

    Microbubble aeration was applied to raise dissolved oxygen in the root zone, which has a direct impact on how fast the plants grow.

  5. In progress — Biofloc Line

    Biofloc ponds for tilapia are being prepared, along with a plan to connect them to the greenhouse as an aquaponics system. See the plan.
